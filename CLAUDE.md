# Project overview
The Cognitive Load Reducer (CLR)
AI that reduces the mental overhead of modern life by acting as a “cognitive firewall.”
Humans are drowning in:

notifications

decisions

micro‑tasks

information overload

This AI acts as a buffer between you and the world.

It would:

Filter incoming information

Rewrite messages into simpler forms

Auto‑summarize everything

Predict what you actually need to know

Handle low‑value decisions automatically

Create a “mental bandwidth score”

Suggest ways to reduce cognitive load

Why it matters  
Cognitive overload is a silent global crisis.
Reducing it improves productivity, mental health, and creativity.
